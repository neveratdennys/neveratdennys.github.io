## AI Safety

My current interest is AI safety in a fairly concrete sense: how to notice when a model is strategically hiding intent, when a behavior is only superficially aligned, and how to build evaluations that make those failures easier to study. I am especially interested in work that connects interpretability to usable monitoring signals.
